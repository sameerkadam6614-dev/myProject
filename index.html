Rooted in Values, Empowered for the Future

The Academic Year 2026–27 marks another significant milestone in the journey of Khedekar College of Arts, Science and Commerce. As we continue to embrace the transformative vision of the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, now fully implemented across undergraduate and postgraduate programmes, we are witnessing a new era of higher education that emphasizes flexibility, multidisciplinary learning, skill development, innovation, and holistic growth.

Guided by our autonomous status and driven by a commitment to academic excellence, we have continually expanded opportunities for our students to prepare for the rapidly evolving global landscape. This year, we are proud to introduce several future-focused programmes, including B.Com. in International Accounting integrated with ACCA, B.Sc. Cyber Security, M.Sc. Cyber Security, and a Postgraduate Diploma in Drone Operations. These programmes have been thoughtfully designed to equip students with industry-relevant competencies, global perspectives, and emerging technological skills that are increasingly sought after in today’s dynamic professional environment.

As I reflect upon our institution’s journey, I am reminded of the banyan tree—a symbol of wisdom, strength, resilience, and continuous growth. Its deep roots provide stability, while its expanding branches reach towards new horizons. Similarly, our college remains firmly grounded in its core values while continuously adapting to the changing educational ecosystem. Every student who joins our institution becomes part of this living legacy, nurtured through knowledge, values, experiences, and opportunities that empower them to flourish independently and contribute meaningfully to society.

Established in 1963, R J College has proudly carried forward a rich tradition of academic excellence for over six decades. Our autonomous status, granted by the University of Mumbai and UGC in 2018, has enabled us to design innovative curricula, adopt contemporary pedagogies, and respond swiftly to the emerging needs of industry and society. Our recent achievement of securing an ‘A’ Grade with a CGPA of 3.10 in the Fourth Cycle of NAAC Reaccreditation further reflects our unwavering commitment to quality education and institutional development.
