ABOUT US

Hindi Vidya Prachar Samiti’s Ramniranjan Jhunjhunwala College of Arts, Science and Commerce with an Empowered Autonomous Status was established in 1963 by a visionary Postman Shri Nandkishore Singh Jairmaji to provide quality education to the youth.

In a span of six decades the college has scaled several milestones in keeping with the rapidly changing needs of society. In the twentieth century institutional boundaries of states and countries have vanished, and education has become global.

Ramniranjan Jhunjhunwala College with a beginning as a conventional Higher Educational Institution soon realized that students must have access to professional education and must be trained to have the employability skills which the industries are looking for.

In the last two decades, the college has introduced several programs to provide students with greater opportunity for selecting the program of their choice.

Library

A college library is an important place for students to study, learn, and gain knowledge. It provides a quiet and peaceful environment where students can read books, prepare for examinations, complete assignments, and conduct research.

A good college library contains textbooks, reference books, newspapers, magazines, journals, and digital resources on different subjects. It may also provide computers, internet access, e-books, and online databases.

The library helps students develop good reading habits, improve their knowledge, and become more independent learners. Therefore, a well-equipped college library plays an important role in the academic success and overall development of students.

College Event

College events are an important part of student life. They provide students with opportunities to learn new skills, show their talents, and interact with their classmates.

Colleges organize various events such as cultural programs, sports competitions, seminars, workshops, festivals, debates, and annual functions.

These events help students develop confidence, teamwork, leadership, communication, and organizational skills. They also create a friendly and enjoyable atmosphere on campus.

College events allow students to take a break from their studies while gaining valuable experiences. Therefore, college events play an important role in the overall development and personality of students.
